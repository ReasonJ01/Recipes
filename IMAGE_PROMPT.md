# Recipe image house prompt

Use this as the default prompt for generated recipe images unless a recipe needs something different.

Create a vertical 2:3 recipe image in the house style for recipes.reason.place.

- Warm parchment or lightly aged paper background.
- Subtle fantasy herbarium / bestiary / old culinary field-guide feel.
- Realistic food rendering with a slightly illustrated, old-book quality.
- Soft warm lighting and restrained, natural colour.
- Place the food low in frame with generous negative space above.
- Keep the composition sparse: no people, text, plates, cutlery, or unrelated props.
- A few crumbs, sugar crystals, herbs, peel, or other natural debris are fine when they belong to the dish.
- Show the recipe's defining visual details clearly and accurately.
- If the interior matters, cut or break one portion and place it in front or beside the main dish so the crumb, filling, or layers are visible.
- Garnishes should be minimal and specific to the recipe.
- The result should feel like a specimen documented in an old herbary or bestiary, but still appetising and believable as food.

Prompt ending:

`Generate [RECIPE NAME] in this style, showing [KEY VISUAL DETAILS]. No text.`
