---
title: "Lemon Drizzle Loaf with Crunchy Sugar Crust & Caramelised Lemon"
---

## Ingredients
- Cake: 225 g unsalted butter, softened
- Cake: 225 g caster sugar
- Cake: 4 large eggs, room temperature
- Cake: 225 g self-raising flour
- Cake: Finely grated zest of 1 unwaxed lemon
- Cake: 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Tin: About 15 g soft butter
- Tin: 35–40 g demerara sugar
- Crunchy lemon drizzle: 40 g fresh lemon juice
- Crunchy lemon drizzle: 100 g granulated sugar
- Candied caramelised lemons: 1 small unwaxed lemon
- Candied caramelised lemons: 100 g caster sugar
- Candied caramelised lemons: 100 ml water
- Candied caramelised lemons: 10–15 g caster or granulated sugar, for caramelising

## Instructions
1. Start the lemon slices first. Slice the small lemon into rounds about 3 mm thick, remove the seeds, and select 4–6 good central slices.
2. Put 100 g caster sugar and 100 ml water into a wide pan and bring to the boil. Add the lemon slices, reduce to a steady gentle simmer, and cook for 10–15 minutes, until the flesh and white pith look translucent.
3. Transfer the lemon slices to a wire rack to drain and dry.
4. Heat the oven to 180°C conventional / 160°C fan.
5. Use the loaf tin without parchment. Butter the entire inside generously and evenly, including the corners, with about 15 g soft butter. Add 35–40 g demerara sugar, tilt and rotate the tin until the base and all four sides are thoroughly coated, and tip out the excess. Optionally chill the prepared tin for 10 minutes.
6. Beat 225 g softened butter and 225 g caster sugar for 3–5 minutes, until paler and fluffy.
7. Beat in the 4 eggs one at a time.
8. Add the finely grated zest of 1 lemon and 1 tsp vanilla extract and mix briefly.
9. Fold in 225 g self-raising flour and stop as soon as no dry flour remains.
10. Spoon the batter carefully into the tin so as not to disturb the sugar coating. Gently level it and fill the tin only 2/3 full.
11. Bake on the middle shelf, checking from 35 minutes, until golden brown and a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean.
12. Make the crunchy drizzle only when the cake is almost finished baking. Very briefly mix 40 g fresh lemon juice with 100 g granulated sugar; it should remain a thick, gritty slurry with lots of clearly visible sugar crystals.
13. When the cake comes out of the oven, leave it in the tin for about 5 minutes, then prick the top deeply with a thin skewer.
14. Give the drizzle another very brief stir and spoon it over the hot cake. Finish with visible sugar sitting on top and do not smooth it. Keep as much drizzle as possible off the sides so the demerara exterior remains crisp.
15. Leave the cake for about 8–10 minutes, loosen the edges, turn it out, and cool completely on a rack.
16. Check the candied lemon slices. They should be translucent, flexible, no longer dripping, and tacky rather than soaking wet.
17. Put the best 3–4 slices on a plate and sprinkle one side lightly with 10–15 g caster or granulated sugar.
18. Heat a good non-stick frying pan over medium to medium-high heat. Put the lemon slices in sugared-side down and cook until deeply caramelised, with deep amber or dark caramel patches.
19. Transfer the slices to baking parchment or a silicone mat and leave to cool.
20. Once both the loaf and lemon slices are completely cool, arrange 2–3 caramelised lemon slices along the central crack.

## Notes
- Keep the leftover syrup from candying the lemons to make homemade lemonade.
