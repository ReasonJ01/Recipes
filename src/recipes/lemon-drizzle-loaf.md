---
title: "Lemon Drizzle Loaf with Crunchy Sugar Crust & Caramelised Lemon"
---

## Ingredients
- Cake: 225 g unsalted butter, softened
- Cake: 225 g caster sugar
- Cake: 4 large eggs, room temperature
- Cake: 225 g self-raising flour
- Cake: Finely grated zest of 1 unwaxed lemon
- Cake: 1 tsp vanilla extract
- Tin: About 15 g soft butter
- Tin: 35–40 g demerara sugar
- Crunchy lemon drizzle: 40 g fresh lemon juice
- Crunchy lemon drizzle: 100 g granulated sugar
- Candied caramelised lemons: 1 small unwaxed lemon
- Candied caramelised lemons: 100 g caster sugar
- Candied caramelised lemons: 100 ml water
- Candied caramelised lemons: 10–15 g caster or granulated sugar, for caramelising

## Instructions
1. Slice the small lemon into rounds about 3 mm thick, remove the seeds, and select 4–6 central slices.
2. Bring 100 g caster sugar and 100 ml water to the boil in a wide pan. Add the lemon slices and simmer gently for 10–15 minutes until translucent.
3. Transfer the lemon slices to a wire rack to drain and dry.
4. Heat the oven to 180°C conventional / 160°C fan.
5. Butter the inside of the loaf tin with about 15 g soft butter. Add 35–40 g demerara sugar, tilt to coat the base and sides, and tip out the excess. Optionally chill for 10 minutes.
6. Beat 225 g softened butter and 225 g caster sugar for 3–5 minutes until pale and fluffy.
7. Beat in the 4 eggs one at a time.
8. Mix in the lemon zest and 1 tsp vanilla extract.
9. Fold in 225 g self-raising flour until no dry flour remains.
10. Spoon the batter carefully into the tin so as not to disturb the sugar coating. Level gently and fill the tin only 2/3 full.
11. Bake on the middle shelf, checking from 35 minutes. It is done when golden brown and a skewer inserted into the centre comes out clean.
12. Near the end of baking, briefly mix 40 g fresh lemon juice with 100 g granulated sugar, keeping the mixture gritty.
13. Leave the baked loaf in the tin for 5 minutes, then prick the top deeply with a thin skewer.
14. Spoon the drizzle over the hot cake, finishing with the grittiest sugar on top. Keep the drizzle mostly off the sides.
15. Leave for 8–10 minutes, loosen the edges, turn out, and cool completely on a rack.
16. Sprinkle one side of 3–4 candied lemon slices lightly with the 10–15 g sugar.
17. Heat a non-stick frying pan over medium to medium-high heat and fry the slices sugared-side down until deeply caramelised. Transfer to baking parchment or a silicone mat to cool.
18. Arrange 2–3 caramelised lemon slices on the cooled loaf.

## Notes
- Keep the leftover lemon-candying syrup to make homemade lemonade.
