## Ingredients
- 100 g butter
- 100 g white sugar
- 75 g brown sugar
- 1 egg
- 1½–2 tsp vanilla extract
- 115 g flour
- 40 g cocoa powder
- ½ tsp baking soda
- ¼ tsp baking powder
- Pinch of salt
- 100–150 g white chocolate chips
- 50 g dark chocolate, chopped

## Instructions
1. Preheat the oven to 160°C.
2. Cream the 100 g butter, 100 g white sugar and 75 g brown sugar together until light and fluffy.
3. Beat in the egg, then mix in the 1½–2 tsp vanilla extract.
4. Add the 115 g flour, 40 g cocoa powder, ½ tsp baking soda, ¼ tsp baking powder and pinch of salt. Mix until just a few faint streaks of flour remain.
5. Add the 100–150 g white chocolate chips and 50 g chopped dark chocolate, then mix until incorporated.
6. Scoop the dough onto a baking tray.
7. Bake at 160°C for 12–15 minutes.
